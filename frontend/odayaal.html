⭐ Soo bandhigida odayaasha, culimada, aqoonyahannada iyo haldoorka Beesha Moolkaal

🌟 Magacyada Odayaasha & Haldoorka

Beeshu waxay leedahay odayaal iyo haldoor isku jira oo u adeegaya bulshada.:

  • Dr. Cabdulqadir Cabdi Nuur (Falastiin) – Oday dhakhtar ah, oo ku takhasusay caafimaadka, isla markaana ka tirsan kooxda daryeelka bulshada.
  • Maxamed Xasan Mahdi (Afyare) – Oday dhaqameed ka tirsan beesha, ku caan ah midnimada iyo talo bixinta.
  • Cismaan Cabdullaahi Maxaad – Shiikh iyo oday ka tirsan beesha, isagoo sanado badan ku dhaqmay nabadaynta, isku xirka, iyo wadajirka bulshada, isla markaana lagu kalsoon yahay arrimaha diinta iyo dhex-dhexaadinta dadweynaha..
  • Yusuf Cabdi (Yusuf Gaab) – Oday haldoor ah oo ka shaqeeya wadajirka iyo horumarinta bulshada.
  • Dr. Axmed Bashiir Isaakh (DR Bashka) – Dhakhtar iyo haldoor ka tirsan bahda caafimaadka, isagoo sanado badan u soo adeegay bulshada, si daacad ah iyo khibrad lehna uga shaqeeya daryeelka iyo caafimaadka dadweynaha, isla markaana door muhiim ah ka ciyaara horumarinta adeegga caafimaadka iyo wacyigelinta bulshada..

🤝 Doorkooda Bulshada

Odayaashani waxay ka shaqeeyaan:

  • 🤝 Midnimada iyo wadajirka bulshada
  • 🕌 Ilaalinta dhaqanka iyo diinta
  • 🏥 Daryeelka iyo horumarinta caafimaadka
  • 📚 Talo bixinta iyo hagidda jiilasha cusub

🌱 Gunaanad

Odayaasha iyo haldoorka beesha waa astaan u ah horumar, aqoon iyo midnimo. Waxay door muhiim ah ka qaataan dhismaha bulshada iyo mustaqbalka jiilasha soo socda.
